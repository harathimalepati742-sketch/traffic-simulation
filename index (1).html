🚦 Traffic Rules Game

Follow the rules to earn points. Break them and you lose points!

🚗 Driving rules

  • Fasten your seat belt before moving (+5 / −5)
  • Stop at a red light (+5), never run it (−15)
  • Pass on green (+10)
  • Give way to people on a zebra crossing (+10 / −20)
  • Obey the speed limit, 30 km/h in school zones (−3)
  • Don't crash into other cars (−20)

🚶 Walking rules

  • Look left and right before crossing (−5 if you forget)
  • Cross only on the zebra crossing (−10 if you jaywalk)
  • Wait for the green WALK signal (−15 on red)
  • Reach the other side safely (+15)
  • Perfect crossing with no mistakes (+10 bonus)
  • Getting hit by a car (−20)

You start with 50 points. If you reach 0, the game is over.
